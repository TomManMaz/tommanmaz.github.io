Check a Permutation

Choose a QAPLIB instance, paste a permutation or upload an .sln file, and get its cost compared with the best known value. Everything runs in your browser.

A file named after the instance (e.g. tai30a.sln) selects it automatically.
Numbers 1..n separated by spaces, commas or line breaks; 0-based lists and full .sln files are accepted too.
How the cost is computed

For an instance with matrices a and b (the first and second matrix in the .dat file) and a permutation p, where p(i) is the location of facility i: 

cost(p) = sum over i, j of  a[i][j] * b[p(i)][p(j)]

This is the QAPLIB convention. If your code uses the opposite one, the checker also reports the cost of the inverse permutation.
